    1 astronomical unit = 149,597,870.7 km
    Speed of light = 299,792.458 km/s

All astronomy reference links can be read independently in a browser;
the offline model works without a connection.

CURRENT VECTOR POSITIONS / NASA/JPL Horizons API
https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/horizons.html
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api
Version 1.3. State vectors: heliocentric center 500@10, J2000 ecliptic
ICRF, UT timestamps, KM-S, geometric VEC_CORR=NONE, VEC_TABLE=2.
15-minute samples are interpolated with a cubic Hermite polynomial;
there is no extrapolation. Paths use current-state osculating ellipses when JPL data is available;
otherwise the offline Kepler model is used.
Cached data and fallback sources are labeled per body in the interface.

MEAN SOLAR DAY / SIDEREAL ROTATION
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/
https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/attic/cosmicopia.gsfc.nasa.gov/qa_earth.html
Solar-day estimate: 1 / abs(1 / signed_spin_hours - 1 / year_hours).
For moons, year_hours is the parent world's solar orbital period.
This is a mean approximation; it omits eccentricity and obliquity variations.

Launch/return dates and major milestones are historical, rounded to minutes.
Intermediate visual transitions are approximate educational cues, not precise
telemetry events. Artemis II flew April 1-10, 2026 local dates; splashdown is
April 11 00:07 UTC. It flew around the Moon without orbit capture or landing.
Mission routes, orbital turns, separation and spacecraft positions are
schematic. Replay compression varies by stage to keep short events visible.
No historical spacecraft state vectors or N-body flight dynamics are used.

LIVE LAUNCH MONITOR / Launch Library 2.3.0
https://thespacedevs.com/llapi
https://github.com/TheSpaceDevs/Tutorials/blob/main/faqs/faq_LL2.md
https://ll.thespacedevs.com/2.3.0/launches/
https://ll.thespacedevs.com/2.3.0/launches/upcoming/
https://ll.thespacedevs.com/2.3.0/config/launch_statuses/
The production feed supplies statuses, UTC NET, agency, rocket, pad and
webcast metadata. Development/stale lldev data is not used. Free access
is 15 calls/hour/IP; normal refreshes use a 15-minute cache. Status ID 6
is reported in flight. Countdown timing is not telemetry. NASA grouping
uses provider/mission/program agencies; ESA/Europe also includes European
Ariane/Vega operators, preserving provider names. Upcoming matches come
from the next 100 global schedule entries. No synthetic live paths.
